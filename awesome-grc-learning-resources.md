# Awesome GRC Learning Resources
![](images/awesome-grc-learning-resources.png)

GRC (Governance, Risk and Compliance) is the policy/process/control-oriented side of cybersecurity. It touches frameworks (ISO 27001, SOC 2, NIST CSF, PCI DSS), risk quantification, third-party/vendor risk, privacy (GDPR, DPDP, CCPA), and audit. This list is biased toward practitioners who want to break into or grow in security GRC/risk roles.

## Books
1. [Measuring and Managing Information Risk: A FAIR Approach by Jack Freund and Jack Jones](https://www.sciencedirect.com/book/9780124202313/measuring-and-managing-information-risk) - The foundational text on quantitative risk with FAIR.
2. [How to Measure Anything in Cybersecurity Risk by Douglas Hubbard, Richard Seiersen](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/How+to+Measure+Anything+in+Cybersecurity+Risk%2C+2nd+Edition-p-9781119892304) - Essential critique of qualitative risk and how to replace it.
3. [ISO 27001 Handbook - A Step-by-Step Guide by Cees van der Wens](https://www.amazon.com/ISO-27001-Handbook-Implementing-Companies/dp/1712622404)
4. [IT Auditing Using Controls to Protect Information Assets by Chris Davis, Mike Schiller](https://www.mhprofessional.com/9781260453225-usa-it-auditing-using-controls-to-protect-information-assets-third-edition)
5. [The Security Risk Assessment Handbook by Douglas Landoll](https://www.routledge.com/The-Security-Risk-Assessment-Handbook-A-Complete-Guide-for-Performing-Security-Risk-Assessments/Landoll/p/book/9781032041254)
6. [CISA Review Manual (ISACA)](https://www.isaca.org/credentialing/cisa/cisa-review-manual) - For auditors.
7. [CISSP Official Study Guide (Sybex)](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/CISSP+Official+Study+Guide) - Covers domain 1 (Security & Risk Management) in depth.
8. [PCI DSS v4.0.1 Standard (free)](https://www.pcisecuritystandards.org/document_library/) - Read it cover to cover if you work with card data.

## Certifications
1. [CISSP by ISC2](https://www.isc2.org/certifications/cissp) - Broad, includes risk/governance.
2. [CGRC by ISC2](https://www.isc2.org/certifications/cgrc) - Formerly CAP; governance/risk/compliance focus.
3. [CISA by ISACA](https://www.isaca.org/credentialing/cisa) - IT audit flagship.
4. [CRISC by ISACA](https://www.isaca.org/credentialing/crisc) - Risk & information systems control.
5. [CGEIT by ISACA](https://www.isaca.org/credentialing/cgeit) - Enterprise IT governance.
6. [CISM by ISACA](https://www.isaca.org/credentialing/cism) - Information security management.
7. [ISO 27001 Lead Implementer / Lead Auditor (PECB / BSI)](https://pecb.com/) - Framework-specific.
8. [Open FAIR Certification (The Open Group)](https://www.opengroup.org/certifications/openfair)
9. [CDPSE by ISACA](https://www.isaca.org/credentialing/cdpse) - Privacy engineering.
10. [CIPP/E, CIPP/US, CIPM by IAPP](https://iapp.org/certify/) - Privacy professional certs.
11. [HITRUST CCSFP](https://hitrustalliance.net/) - Healthcare-focused compliance.
12. [PCIP / PCI ISA / PCI QSA](https://www.pcisecuritystandards.org/program_training_and_qualification/) - Card-industry specific.
